# Mission 3 Reflection

This laboratory activity helped me understand the differences between AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. Before doing this activity, I mostly knew these platforms as cloud providers, but I did not know how their services compared with each other. After researching them, I realized that they offer many of the same types of services, but each provider has areas where it can be a better choice.

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was **Google Cloud**. I found its focus on artificial intelligence, data analytics, and Kubernetes interesting. Google Kubernetes Engine was also something that caught my attention because Kubernetes is an important technology for modern cloud applications. However, I also learned that AWS and Azure are very strong platforms and can be better choices depending on the situation.

AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud have many similarities. All three provide virtual machines, object storage, databases, networking, identity management, security services, and tools for application development. They also use regions and other infrastructure locations to help organizations deploy their applications closer to their users.

Business requirements are very important when selecting a cloud platform. For example, a company that already depends heavily on Microsoft products may benefit more from Azure. A company focused on AI and machine learning may prefer Google Cloud, while an organization that needs a broad range of cloud services may consider AWS.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me that choosing a cloud provider should not be based only on popularity. I need to understand the client's budget, existing systems, workload, security requirements, scalability needs, and future plans before making a recommendation.
